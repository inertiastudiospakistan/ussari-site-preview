AI Receptionist Demo

A missed-call receptionist for a home-services business. The business missed a call — the receptionist answers, takes the job details, and books it into the day’s schedule. Try it: run a simulated call below.

Read this first — what this demo is

This is a TEXT demo of the receptionist flow. No live phone call is placed, no number is dialled, and no SMS is actually sent. The business, the caller and the bookings on this page are samples.

Sample business: “Sample Plumbing Co.” — a fictional plumbing & HVAC business created for this demo. It is not a real client of Ussari Labs.

A live voice deployment would run on a voice platform such as Vapi, Retell or Bland, with the client paying usage at ~$0.13–$0.14 per minute all-in. This demo itself runs entirely in your browser at $0 — nothing you type leaves this page, and nothing is stored.

How a live version would work

AUDIT FIRST, THEN BUILD
  1. Audit: Ussari maps the business’s real missed calls, services, service area and booking rules before building anything.
  2. Build: the receptionist is deployed on a voice platform such as Vapi, Retell or Bland, connected to the business’s real calendar and phone number.
  3. Run: it answers missed calls in voice, books into the real schedule, logs every call and sends a real SMS confirmation. The client pays usage at ~$0.13–$0.14 per minute all-in.

What this demo does — and does not do

  • Does: run the full booking conversation in text; understand free-text answers; ask again when an answer is missing or unclear; fill real slots; refuse a double-booking and offer the nearest free alternative; log the call; preview the SMS.
  • Does not: place or receive a real phone call; send a real SMS; connect to any calendar, phone system or voice platform; use any real business, caller or booking. No data leaves your browser.
