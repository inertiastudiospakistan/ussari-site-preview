Inbox / CRM Triage

Paste an incoming message. The demo sorts it, scores its urgency, drafts a reply — and files it on a CRM board. Everything below runs in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Demo — rule-based triage. This page classifies with keyword / signal scoring in JavaScript (see “How it works” below). A live deployment would use an LLM via the client’s own account, and the client pays that usage. The four samples are invented examples, labelled SAMPLE — they are not real customers, and there are no real client results, testimonials, or contact details on this page. The board lives only in this browser tab and resets when the page is reloaded.

  1. How it works — the classifier scores the message against four signal lists (lead, complaint, supplier, spam). The highest score wins; a strong spam signal always wins, because a prize / crypto / “click here” message should never be answered as a lead. Ties break in the order spam > complaint > lead > supplier.
  2. Urgency 1–5 — starts at 1 and adds points for urgent words (+2), angry tone (+1), money mentioned (+1), a deadline or date (+1), and a blocked / not-working service (+1), capped at 5. Spam is always urgency 1 — it is deprioritised, not answered.
  3. Drafts — templates filled with details pulled from the message itself: amounts (Rs / $ / £), invoice or order references, dates, and the words that triggered the classification. No name is invented — the draft opens with “Hello,” unless the message itself contains one.
  4. Limits — this is keyword scoring, not understanding. Sarcasm, mixed messages (“great service” said angrily), and new wording it has no signals for will be misclassified. That is exactly what the audit-first step of a live deployment is for: the client’s real inbox is sampled first, and the rules — or the LLM prompt — are tuned to it.
