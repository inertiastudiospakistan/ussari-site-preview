Mobile  /  Product

Saudi EOS Calculator

End-of-service award calculator for workers in Saudi Arabia, built on Articles 84 and 85 of the Saudi Labour Law.

  • Build 8 AAB 3.35 MB
  • 0 permissions — fully offline
Full detail

Worked example: 10,000 SAR monthly wage, 7 years of service, employment ended by the employer — the award is 45,000 SAR. Resignation applies Article 85 tiers (none under 2 years, one third 2–5, two thirds 5–10, full at 10+). Kotlin / Jetpack Compose, MVI, navy + amber, target SDK 36, Arabic toggle, history. The result is an estimate, not legal advice. Play Store listing in progress — not live yet.

AI infrastructure

The harness

Routing gateway that puts free AI models behind one interface — score-based routing, circuit breakers, OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible adapters.

  • 199 endpoints tracked
  • 32 enabled, live-tested
Full detail

In OpenCode there is exactly one model — “harness” — and all provider and model switching happens inside the gateway, with profiles, SQLite state and a /dashboard. Enabled providers verified live include OVH AI Endpoints (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, 1M context), Cohere (Command A, 256K), Inferbase and AnyAPI (Nemotron-3-Ultra, 1M). No token volume or revenue is claimed for it.
