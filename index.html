A little about us

Three founders. One shared approach to building better software.

Ussari Labs was founded by three friends — Adil Warraich, Qazi Shafee, and Kamran Khan.

We started with a simple belief: businesses shouldn’t need enterprise-sized budgets to access thoughtfully engineered software.

The name Ussari comes from the Punjabi word Usaari, meaning to build or construct from the ground up.

That’s exactly how we approach our work.

We believe in understanding problems before writing code, making sensible engineering decisions, and building products that businesses can actually use.

We’re a small, hands-on engineering studio based in Bahawalpur, Pakistan.

When you work with us, you work directly with the people responsible for building your product.

We don’t want to be the biggest development company. We want to be the team you can confidently trust with your next project.

You bring us the problem. We bring the engineering. Together, we build the solution.
