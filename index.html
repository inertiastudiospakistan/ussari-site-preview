Mobile  /  Product

Saudi EOS Calculator

End-of-service award calculator for workers in Saudi Arabia, built on Articles 84 and 85 of the Saudi Labour Law.

Worked example: 10,000 SAR monthly wage, 7 years of service, employment ended by the employer — the award is 45,000 SAR. Resignation applies Article 85 tiers (none under 2 years, one third 2–5, two thirds 5–10, full at 10+). Kotlin / Jetpack Compose, MVI, target SDK 36, Arabic toggle, history. The result is an estimate, not legal advice.

FAQ

Asked, answered.

What does it cost?

It depends on the scope — that is what the free audit is for. A fixed-scope build is priced in writing, before any code, after scoping. The AI automation pilot is a two-week paid pilot on one workflow, and its price is set in the audit, in writing. No open-ended billing.

How long does a build take?

A pilot runs 2 weeks. A full build depends on scope — the audit gives you a timeline in writing. We do not quote a date we cannot keep.

Who owns the code?

You do — the code, the data and the documentation, with no lock-in. It is yours from day one.

Do you work with hospitals?

Yes — we build hospital systems covering registration, OPD, lab, dialysis, OT and radiology. The work section above shows the system.

What does the free audit cover?

A look at how your business actually runs day to day — the calls, the emails, the follow-ups — and a plain-English map of where time leaks and what is worth automating. Nothing for you to prepare.

What technologies do you build with?

Python and FastAPI, React, Kotlin / Jetpack Compose, Neon Postgres — picked per project, not per fashion. The stack line in the services section above lists exactly what the studio’s products already run on.
